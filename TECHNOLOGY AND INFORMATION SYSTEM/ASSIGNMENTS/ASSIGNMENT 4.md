  <h2> 📝 Summarization of the visit : </h2>

  From the ICT department I learned: 

<h3>🌐 ICT</h3>
Information and communication technology is essential in the ports and logistics industry to support efficient, fast, and user-friendly services. At Johor Port, ICT plays a crucial role in integrating daily operational and management activities, making it an important business tool for ensuring effective connectivity.
<h3>📊 4 Main Pillars : </h3>

🏗️ Infrastructure : 
* Johor Port has two data centres connected by networks and uses both wired and wireless connections. A broadband fibre network covers about 60%–70% of the port area over approximately 50 km. Non-disruptive upgrades help minimise planned downtime, while automated site failover reduces unplanned downtime and ensures continuous availability.
* The network is supported by two main switches, which act as the core of the network, along with additional firewalls at the server level. Johor Port currently uses physical UNIX servers. Wi-Fi coverage is available both indoors and outdoors, supported by a 4G LTE network.
* Johor Port uses Oracle for storage because of its stability in handling data.

💼 Business application : 
- Johor Port uses ERP RAMCO for business operations. It also uses PAMS (Port Access Management System) to manage access, permits, and security for vehicles and people entering the port area.
-  low-code platforms:  primarily used to enable faster and more flexible development of internal workflows and business process applications that are not handled by strategic or backend systems.
- Data analysis: Johor Port uses well-presented data to analyse information and present it to the CEO, allowing management to quickly view profit and make informed decisions.


📈 Project and Governance
- Project and governance is how a team plans and organizes work while following rules and policies. An (ISMS) based on ISO/IEC 27001 supports this by protecting important information, setting security rules, and managing risks.

📋 Admin & Helpdesk
* Admin & Helpdesk are parts of IT support. ICT Administration manages and maintains IT systems like computers and software to keep them running smoothly while ICT Helpdesk helps users fix problems with their computers, or other IT tools.
  
<h3>🔴 Issue</h3>
Johor Port aims to catch up with the Port of Singapore, which operates at a faster pace. The port currently uses physical servers and is looking towards cloud servers for easier management. Additionally, Johor Port wants to improve PAMS by integrating IoT, enabling RFID-based access instead of manual security checks.

<h2>✏️ Reflection : </h2>
The visit to Johor Port exposed me to how innovation and ICT are applied in a highly secure industrial environment. The advanced security systems and continuous monitoring processes highlighted the importance of technology in ensuring safety, efficiency, and smooth port operations. This experience increased my appreciation of how technological innovation supports human life by protecting critical infrastructure and enabling reliable services for businesses and society.

<h2>💡 Suggestion improvement : </h2>
The visit should show more detailed demonstration of PAMS or JPBI so that students can understand more on how it is implemented in real world application.
